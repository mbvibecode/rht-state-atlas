A key interpretive limit

Digital infrastructure and behavioral health initiatives can appear in the same plan without establishing that behavioral health providers receive the technology or participate in shared workflows.

Follow-up examples: Utah names BH data connections; Rhode Island describes technology-supported community hubs; Kansas links BH with primary care, emergency departments, and nursing facilities; South Dakota pairs CCBHC and Collaborative Care proposals with digital investments. These are illustrative designs for further review, not confirmed operational models.

Sources, dates, and review method

State summaries and proposal classifications are analyst-curated from the December 2025 state abstract compilation. General needs or possible recipients alone do not qualify as a proposed activity. The reconciliation checks each theme independently against positive-dollar official categories, initiative titles, and line-item descriptions in the obligations report. Care-area + technology linkage requires both concepts in one positive-dollar line-item/category segment, or a care-specific initiative title governing the technology line item. State-, page-, and broad-initiative co-occurrence alone do not qualify. Zero-dollar initiatives are excluded. The obligation classifications were checked through the original positive-queue review and a supplemental negative-gap audit; all current positive cells are covered by the adjudication table in the Excel inventory.

The obligations report covers December 29, 2025–July 31, 2026; its October filename is not the data cutoff. “Proposed only” means the theme was named in the state abstract but not explicit in that snapshot; it does not mean the activity was cancelled. Obligations do not establish expenditure, operational services, or outcomes. Broad technology categories are not used to infer AI, cybersecurity, telehealth, or data subtypes.

The supplied press-release workbook indexes headlines through October 9, 2026. It is included in Excel as context; headlines and their amounts are not combined with obligations. The internal project briefs guide the BH/technology lens. The resource guides and funding fact sheets inform interpretation rather than state coding.

The Excel inventory contains the complete codebook, abstract text, supporting excerpts, source hashes, obligation detail, and state-by-theme reconciliation. Supporting source PDFs and CSV downloads are published alongside this page; no external scripts, fonts, or map services are required.
